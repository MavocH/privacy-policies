Privacy Policy — Daredevil Dodo

Daredevil Dodo is a single-player, offline game. This policy is short because there isn't much to say: the app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, from anyone, ever.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information, device identifiers, or usage analytics.
  • It does not contain ads or any ad SDK.
  • It does not contain crash-reporting, analytics, or other third-party tracking SDKs.
  • It does not require or create an account, sign-in, or profile.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request permission to access the internet at all, so it has no technical means of sending data anywhere even if it wanted to.
  • It does not use cookies (it isn't a website, and has no web views).
  • It is not directed at children specifically, but contains nothing unsuitable for them and collects nothing from players of any age.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves a small settings file locally on your own device, in the standard way Android apps store simple settings, containing only:

  • your high score,
  • your sound and graphics-style preferences (on/off, retro/HD), and
  • the name you last typed in for the optional "share your score" card (blank until you type one).

None of this ever leaves your device. It is not uploaded, synced, backed up to a server, or visible to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The optional share feature

If you choose to tap "Share" after a run, the app generates an image (your name, score, and a snapshot of the scene) and hands it to Android's own share sheet — the same system dialog every app uses to share to Messages, email, or any other app you pick. The app itself does not send this image anywhere; where it ends up is entirely up to the app you choose in that share sheet, just as with any photo or file you share from your device. You can decline to use this feature at any time; it plays no part in the game itself.
