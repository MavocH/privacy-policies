EneaSaga – Privacy Policy

In short: EneaSaga does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and does not use the internet.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, EneaSaga saves a few values in the app's private storage on your device: your best score, the furthest level you have reached, which chapters you have won, the hero you picked and whether sound is on. These values never leave your device through the app, and nobody – including the developer – can see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Device backup

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup, so that it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is performed by Android and your Google account under your own settings; the developer has no access to it.

Sharing a picture of your run

At the end of a run you can choose to share a picture of it. The picture is created on your device and handed to the app you pick in Android's share menu (for example a messenger). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share, and then only to the app you chose.

No data collection, no third parties

  • No personal information is collected or transmitted.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases.
  • No third-party services or SDKs that collect data.
  • The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other sensitive permission.
