Eneas' WRDZ – Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

In short: Eneas' WRDZ does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and does not use the internet.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, Eneas' WRDZ saves a few values in the app's private storage on your device: your settings (languages, difficulty, keyboard, sky, sound and vibration), your statistics and trophies, the words in your album with their stars, today's daily-word result and the last words you played, so that they don't repeat too soon. These values never leave your device through the app, and nobody – including the developer – can see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them, and Settings → Reset progress erases them at any time.

Device backup

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup, so that it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is performed by Android and your Google account under your own settings; the developer has no access to it.

Reading words aloud

The game can pronounce words. It does this with the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example Google's speech services): the app hands the engine only the word to be spoken, such as "le papillon". No information about you is passed along. The engine is part of your device, and its own terms apply to it. You can switch this off under Settings → Read words aloud.

Sharing your daily-word result

After the daily word you can choose to share your result, a short line of text such as "Eneas' WRDZ · Daily #12 🇬🇧 — solved with 1 miss 🎈🎈🎈🎈🎈💥". The text is handed to the app you pick in Android's share menu (for example a messenger). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share, and then only to the app you chose.

Two-player mode

The secret word and clue typed in two-player mode stay in the app's memory for that round only. They are not saved and never leave the device.

Children

Eneas' WRDZ is made for the whole family, including young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There is no chat, no contact with other players, no links to buy anything and no advertising.

No data collection, no third parties

  • No personal information is collected or transmitted.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases.
  • No third-party services or SDKs that collect data.
  • The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other sensitive permission. Its only permission is vibration, for the gentle feedback when you play.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions about it can be sent to the developer at the contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.

Hosting of this page

This is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement, not by Eneas' WRDZ itself: github.com's privacy statement.
